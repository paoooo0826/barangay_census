# Barangay Census update — 5 October 2026

This patch was prepared against GitHub commit `c3eb45cbf0e41137af8955a3effe58ece939d7e4` from `paoooo0826/barangay_census`. Preserve the paths in this archive when replacing or adding files. Unrelated project files and dependencies remain unchanged.

## Changes

- FHONA displays as **Family Head and other needy adults** while existing category values remain compatible.
- Housing tenure accepts **Landlord/Landlady**, including census edits and administrator details.
- Residence periods preserve their original start date and initial classification. Temporary residence becomes Resident after six calendar months using the database's Manila date. Existing records without a known start date show an honest missing-data state; dates are not guessed.
- Admins can record or end residence periods. Ending a period preserves history; a new period starts its own six-month calculation. Moving out of a boarding house does not end barangay residence.
- Admins assign boarding houses to registered owners. Assigned owners can record move-ins using resident tracking numbers and mark departures with a required date. Closed stays remain in history and cannot be overwritten. Owners cannot manage another owner's house.
- Analytics titles describe the four actual demographic charts.
- Dropdowns and dismissible detail panels close outside while preserving inside interaction and existing unsaved-change confirmations.
- Recent Services lists the latest five real appointment/service records and links to searchable service details.
- Active appointments contain Pending and Confirmed requests. Appointment History has Completed and Cancelled tabs; a Rejected tab preserves access to existing rejected records. Completed/cancelled dates and cancellation reasons remain visible.
- Announcements support editing without creating duplicates, safe image replacement, archive/restore, long-text expansion, and periodic refreshing. Failed signed image URLs no longer hide the whole feed. Concurrent stale edits report errors instead of silently overwriting newer records.
